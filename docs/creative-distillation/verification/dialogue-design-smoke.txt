**1. Frayed Ribbon** Blood-red (#8B0000) sweeps diagonally, thinning to pink-gold thread (#E5C100/#FFB7C5); charcoal ground (#2F2F2F) scattered with silver dots (#A8A8A8). Risk: Motion competes with glance-readability. Experiment: Pocket-print it; read while climbing stairs.

**2. Aperture Dial** Charcoal rings (#36454F) pivot around a fixed silver needle (#C0C0C0); pink-gold ticks mark tiers, blood-red fills the center proportionally. Risk: Ring overlap creates moiré at small scale. Experiment: Shrink to 2cm on glass; count distinct rings.

**3. Grid Cascade** Charcoal split diagonally (#2E2E2E); silver blocks (#A6A6A6) stack left, blood-red glyphs slide right, pink-gold lines bridge them. Risk: Diagonal flow opposes standard reading. Experiment: Tape cardboard mockup to a wall; photograph from across the room; check primary count visibility.

Choose Aperture Dial. Radial layout keeps numbers distinct without fighting motion’s pull. Build on removable vinyl first; peel and shift until the eye rests exactly where you need it.
